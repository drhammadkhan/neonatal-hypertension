How the result is worked out

Corrected gestational age is gestation at birth plus age in days, in weeks. The table covers 26 to 44 weeks; outside that range no result is given.

Bands. Below the 50th; 50th up to the 95th; 95th up to the 99th; at or above the 99th. A value equal to a percentile counts as being in the higher band. The overall result is the highest band reached by systolic, diastolic or mean pressure.

Between table rows. For ages between two tabulated rows the percentile values are interpolated in a straight line. For example at 35 weeks the 95th percentile MAP is halfway between the 34-week and 36-week values.

About this tool

Percentile values are from Dionne JM, Abitbol CL, Flynn JT. Hypertension in infancy: diagnosis, management and outcome. Pediatr Nephrol 2012;27:17–32 (values from Table 2 as corrected in the erratum, Pediatr Nephrol 2012;27:159–60), which gives blood pressure by corrected gestational age for infants of 26 to 44 weeks. Plotting a reading and banding it are additions to the published table.

Blood pressure depends on how it was measured, the cuff size and the baby’s state. Use a method consistent with the source, and repeat a high reading before acting on it.

Nothing you enter is sent anywhere.

Intended purpose: shows published blood pressure percentiles for infants by corrected gestational age, and where an entered reading falls against them, for reference. The treating clinician decides what to do. It supports, and does not replace, clinical judgement and current guidance.

Based on Dionne et al., Pediatr Nephrol 2012;27:17–32.
